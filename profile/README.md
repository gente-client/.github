# 🌍 Gente Client

I am a developer who decided to create **Gente Client**, a custom Client designed to improve your Minecraft experience.

We aren't a big group or any else, we just want to give a useful and safe Client to the Minecraft community.

## 🧭 **Our Purpose**

Gente Client was born with the mission of being a lightweight and suitable for everyone with features that really matter. We want to make it able to play lag-free, with better tools that improve your Minecraft experience.

🎮 **What do we offer?**

- *A custom Minecraft client.*

- *Useful PvP features.*

- *Free cosmetics for your skin.*

- *Constant community-based updates so you have better content.*

## 🤝 **How to contribute**

We accept suggestions and bug reports on Discord.
